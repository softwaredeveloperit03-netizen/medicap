Date Of Delivery — Calculation

Formula

Delivery Date = Order Date + Max Material Lead Time (short RMs only) + Production Lead Time

Steps

  1. Standard batch size is taken from unit formula / batch formula for the selected product and plan unit.
  2. Number of batches = Plan Qty ÷ Batch Size.
  3. Required raw material qty is calculated per batch material; stock is checked for RM and mother code.
  4. Net available stock = Total stock − Used (issued) − Booked (WO/MRP allocations) − Hold stock.
  5. Only materials in shortage after net stock check are considered for material lead time; the maximum lead time among those short materials is used.
  6. Production total lead time is taken from Timeline Master → Production for the product.
  7. If all materials are in stock, material lead time = 0 days.
