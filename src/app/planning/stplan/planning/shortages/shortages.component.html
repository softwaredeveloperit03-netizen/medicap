Shortage Analysis — Calculation Rules

Work orders for a material are processed in sequence; the remaining (balance) stock of one work order is carried forward as the available stock of the next. Shortage is calculated on the client material code only (mother-code stock is shown for reference but is not auto-consumed on this screen). No value is ever negative — every result is clamped to a minimum of 0.

1. Material header — stock & PO

Open PO Sum of unreceived PO line qty (po_material.isreceive = no) on POs that are not cancelled/rejected, grouped by client material code.
Mother code available qty Net free stock for the mother code — display only; not used in shortage calculation on this screen.
RM available qty Free client-code pool: stockbook net available plus add-back of open CANNOT_PLAN bookings with indent_status = Not Raised (same as WO-wise). After indent is raised, that booking stays reserved and the row leaves Shortages.
Shortage qtyPer WO: client-code RM shortage after live stock check. Total row sums open shortages.
UnitMaterial UOM.

2. Net in hand formula (client code)

Both RM (client code) and MC (mother code) In-Hand use the same formula. It is the net free stock physically lying in store after removing everything that is already consumed or reserved. It can never go below 0.

Formula Net In Hand = max(0, Available Qty − Booked Qty − Hold Qty)
Available Qty = Total Stock − Issued Qty

What each value means

Total Stock All received & QC-approved (GRN-passed) stock of the material currently recorded in store.
Source: vw_total_available_stock.total_stock_qty
Issued Qty Total quantity already issued / consumed out of that stock (e.g. dispensed to production).
Source: vw_total_available_stock.total_issued_qty
Available Qty Total Stock − Issued Qty — the physical stock still lying in store, before removing reservations / holds.
Source: vw_total_available_stock.available_qty
Booked Qty Stock already reserved for other demand. It is the sum of:
  • Planned WO deductions — deducted_from_RM + deducted_from_MC + deducted_from_Bulk from WO_deductions for active work orders (excludes Cancel / Hold / Rejected).
  • MRP booked stock — qty booked against marketing orders / forecasts (mrp_bookedStock).
Hold Qty Stock that is not usable because it is on hold / quarantine. It is the sum of:
  • Store lots on hold — stock_book rows with status On Hold / Hold.
  • GRN qty on hold — challan_materials.hold_qty where the GRN is On Hold / Hold.
Net In Hand max(0, Available Qty − Booked Qty − Hold Qty) — the free stock truly usable for this material. Shown in the RM available qty column.
